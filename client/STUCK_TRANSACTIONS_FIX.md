# How to Fix Stuck Transactions on FitChain

## Quick Fix Steps:

1. **Use the "Clear Stuck Transactions" button**
   - Go to your Dashboard
   - Look for the yellow "Clear Stuck Transactions" section
   - Click the button to automatically cancel pending transactions

2. **Manual Fix in MetaMask:**
   - Open MetaMask
   - Click on the account menu (3 dots)
   - Go to Settings > Advanced
   - Click "Reset Account" (this only clears transaction history, not your funds)

3. **Speed Up Transactions:**
   - In MetaMask, go to Activity tab
   - Find the pending transaction
   - Click "Speed Up"
   - Increase the gas fee by 50-100%
   - Confirm the new transaction

## Prevention Tips:

1. **Always use higher gas on Sepolia:**
   - The app now automatically adds 20% to gas prices
   - During congestion, manually increase gas in MetaMask

2. **Check gas prices before transacting:**
   - Look for the Gas Price Monitor on your dashboard
   - Avoid transacting when gas is above 50 gwei

3. **One transaction at a time:**
   - Wait for each transaction to confirm before sending another
   - This prevents nonce conflicts
